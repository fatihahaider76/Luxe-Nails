Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Luxe Nails located?+

Luxe Nails is a private home studio in Whitby, Ontario. Since appointments are by request only, the exact address is shared privately once your booking is confirmed.

How do I book an appointment?+

Use the contact form below with your name, contact info, the service you're interested in, and your preferred date. You'll receive a follow-up to confirm your time and studio address.

What's the difference between acrylics and Gel-X?+

Acrylics are a durable powder-and-liquid extension system applied over a tip or sculpted directly on the nail. Gel-X is a lightweight, flexible soft-gel extension that tends to feel more natural. Both are available in a full range of lengths.

Do you sterilize your tools?+

Yes. All tools are thoroughly cleaned and sterilized before every single appointment.

What nail art options do you offer?+

French, ombre, minimal nail art, complex/detailed nail art, and 3D nail art are all available as add-ons — see the pricing menu above.
